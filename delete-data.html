How to request deletion

Send an email with the subject line DELETE MY DATA to:

talhauysal.26@gmail.com

Say which app you are writing about (Mini Mart Tycoon: Farm Shop). You do not need an account and you do not need to prove who you are, because the game does not know who you are. Tell us roughly when you last played and which device you used, so the measurement records can be found.

Every request is answered within 30 days, and deletion is normally done within 7 days of the request.

There is no account to delete

The game has no sign-up, no login, no password and no email address. It is a single-player, offline game: there are no other players, no friends, no visiting and no server of ours that your game talks to. You never create an account, so there is no account to close. What follows is what does exist, and what happens to it.

What is deleted, what is kept

WhatWhere it livesWhat happens
Your game progress — money, unlocked areas, staff, your decorated home, your garage, settings Only on your phone, in the app's private storage We never receive it and cannot delete it for you. You delete it with Settings › Reset Save in the game, by clearing the app's data in Android settings, or by uninstalling the game.
Measurement data — anonymous app-instance identifier, session and screen events, and in-game milestones such as "finished the tutorial" or "unlocked the cafe" Google Analytics for Firebase Deleted on request. It contains no name, no email and nothing you typed. It is also deleted automatically when it reaches the end of its retention period.
Advertising data — your device's advertising identifier and IP address, used by Google AdMob to serve and measure ads Google LLC Held by Google under its own policies, not by us. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Settings › Google › Ads on your device, and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com.
Your email and message if you write to us Our mailbox Kept only as long as needed to answer you, then deleted. Deleted on request.

Nothing is kept for any other reason. There are no purchases, no invoices and no legal records to hold on to, because the game has no in-app purchases.

Stop collection without writing to anyone

  • Play offline. With no internet connection nothing is sent at all — no ads load and no measurement is reported.
  • Uninstall the game. This removes the save file and stops all further collection.
  • Reset your advertising ID in your device settings.
